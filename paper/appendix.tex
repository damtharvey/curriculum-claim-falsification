\subsection{Frozen strategy programs}
\label{app:strategies}

The hash of the frozen rule file, recorded 2026-09-16 20:36:16 UTC before any pass rate was computed, is
\shahex{a0eda4bdb02e54765bb7b49dca5e2e8ea9cd9ebb66de67b2aae074e7d555f0b4}.

The distractor-derivation hub builds a directed graph on option values under a fixed error-transform set and picks the unique maximum out-degree, else lower-central among ties. Constraint elimination uses shallow stem keywords: nonnegative counts, probability in $[0,1]$, percent in $[0,100]$, positive magnitudes, angle ranges, and mean or median inside the listed data. Arithmetic closure at depth two is unbound execution of $+,-,\times,\div$, squares, and square roots on up to 12 stem numbers, with $\pi$ and $ab/2$ on geometry-word stems; it picks the option that first appears, else lower-central among that depth. Backsolving parses a one- or two-variable equation from the stem and picks the unique option that satisfies it. The portfolio runs those four in a fixed order with lower-central among survivors and letter B last.

The machine bar for hub and portfolio is the 95th percentile of 200 random programs of matched complexity, seed 20260916.

The image-channel backsolving addendum was hashed before scoring (sha256 \texttt{d6ee8caf}\ldots). It uses Qwen2-VL-7B-Instruct (Hugging Face revision \texttt{eed13092}\ldots) to transcribe the displayed equation from a page image; substitution is symbolic, outside the model.

\subsection{Language-model scoring}
\label{app:lm}
