\section{Implementation details}
\label{app:implementation}

This appendix records the settings that fix the reported numbers. Nothing here changes a claim in Section~\ref{sec:method}.

\subsection{Frozen strategy programs}
\label{app:strategies}

The hash of the frozen rule file, recorded 2026-09-16 20:36:16 UTC before any pass rate was computed, is
\shahex{a0eda4bdb02e54765bb7b49dca5e2e8ea9cd9ebb66de67b2aae074e7d555f0b4}.

The distractor-derivation hub builds a directed graph on option values under a fixed error-transform set and picks the unique maximum out-degree, else lower-central among ties. Constraint elimination uses shallow stem keywords: nonnegative counts, probability in $[0,1]$, percent in $[0,100]$, positive magnitudes, angle ranges, and mean or median inside the listed data. Arithmetic closure at depth two is unbound execution of $+,-,\times,\div$, squares, and square roots on up to 12 stem numbers, with $\pi$ and $ab/2$ on geometry-word stems; it picks the option that first appears, else lower-central among that depth. Backsolving parses a one- or two-variable equation from the stem and picks the unique option that satisfies it. The portfolio runs those four in a fixed order with lower-central among survivors and letter B last.

The machine bar for hub and portfolio is the 95th percentile of 200 random programs of matched complexity, seed 20260916.

The image-channel backsolving addendum was hashed before scoring; the sha256 of its preregistration is
\shahex{d6ee8cafd35d04bba7bfd4d4d364ee6f274d715e9b6b7020c454dbdfbd994732}.
It uses Qwen2-VL-7B-Instruct (Hugging Face revision \shahex{eed13092ef92e448dd6875b2a00151bd3f7db0ac}) to transcribe the displayed equation from a page image; substitution is symbolic, outside the model.

\paragraph{Option-numeral isomorph control.}
The preregistration of this control was hashed before any scoring for it:
\shahex{997242b9fb7437ffc2e86e2759e1373ba8ad98e231b0836ef8798a2266523e9b}.
Population: the 606 withheld-quantity Algebra~I and Algebra~II items under masking version 1 (358 and 248), with the subsets of Appendices~\ref{app:fidelity} and \ref{app:standards}. Seed 20260916; per item and arm the generator is seeded from the sha256 of seed, arm, and item id, so the substitution does not depend on processing order. Numerals in the option strings are integers (including coefficients and exponents), decimals, comma-grouped integers, and fractions. A replacement keeps the digit count, sign, number of decimal places, leading-dot form, and comma grouping, keeps denominators nonzero, and maps every repeat of the same literal within an item to the same new literal; zero becomes a positive one-digit integer. Variables, operators, units, words, option letters, letter order, and the key letter are unchanged. In the rank-preserving arm the leading numbers of the options are redrawn until their pairwise order equals the original order, ties remaining ties; the scrambled arm has no order constraint. Items with no numeral in any option (61 Algebra~I, 21 Algebra~II) are unchanged under both arms, are included in pass rates, and are excluded from the paired difference. Example, June 2016 Algebra~I item 7: options A to D $6.2$, $7.3$, $8.6$, $8.8$ become $7.4$, $9.6$, $9.7$, $9.8$ in the rank-preserving arm and $8.2$, $4.9$, $4.2$, $2.7$ in the scrambled arm. These invariants were asserted on every item before any perturbed arm was scored. Scoring reuses the masked-stem letter rule above, one model load per model. Original letters are re-scored, not copied, and must agree with the saved per-item predictions on at least $0.98$ of the 606 ids before the perturbed arms are scored: Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct $595/606=0.982$ and Phi-4 $606/606=1.000$ cleared; Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct $592/606=0.977$ did not, so its perturbed arms were not scored. Pass-rate intervals are percentile bootstraps seeded by $21+n$ and paired differences by $31+n$, 1{,}000 replicates each. The pre-registered reading applies to Algebra~I print-faithful items on the rank-preserving arm, each model separately: form-based if the perturbed rate clears the channel bar and the paired difference includes zero; not a form effect if the perturbed rate falls to chance; mixed otherwise. Under that reading, on the text layer, Phi-4 is form-based and Qwen 7B is mixed (Table~\ref{tab:isomorph-text}). The same perturbation, rank-preserving arm only, was then applied to the repaired options of Appendix~\ref{app:repair} under the preregistration of that layer; there the original and the perturbed letters were scored in the same run, so no reproduction floor applies. On that layer Qwen 7B's paired difference includes zero on both cells and Qwen 14B's and Phi-4's exclude it on Algebra~I (Table~\ref{tab:isomorph}). Four repaired items whose perturbation raised or exceeded a 20-second limit are excluded from the perturbed arm and counted.

\subsection{Print-fidelity check}
\label{app:fidelity}

Population: the 955 items in the five cells named in Section~\ref{sec:fidelity-method} (Algebra~I masked-stem 358, Algebra~II masked-stem 248, geometry lower-central fired 279, EQAO grade~6 lower-central fired 40, geometry masked-stem 166; 136 geometry items are in both geometry cells). Transcriber: Qwen2-VL-7B-Instruct, revision \shahex{eed13092ef92e448dd6875b2a00151bd3f7db0ac}, bf16, greedy, 640 new tokens, seed 20260916, asked only to transcribe the stem and options of the numbered item from a 150 DPI render of the source PDF page (Regents: a crop from the question heading to the next heading; EQAO: the full page). The preregistration, with the prompt, the page locator, the normalization, and the thresholds, was hashed before any audited or population item was scored:
\shahex{0211585e9361d3771e04df5e118050ede761971da286f0b1333a7aac289737d6}.

Normalization is identical on both sides: Unicode NFKC, LaTeX commands removed except trigonometric and logarithm names, subscripts and braces removed, unit words to symbols, a space at every letter-digit boundary, alphanumeric tokens, and the tokens for square root, $\pi$, percent, degree, and minus dropped. Control characters and operator glyphs therefore do not count; a digit standing in for an operator does. Stem score is multiset token precision, recall, and F1; option score is 1 for equal compact strings, else the maximum of token F1 and a character-level sequence ratio. Faithful: stem F1, precision, and recall each at least $0.8$, every option at least $0.9$, option counts equal. Unverified: unparseable output, no options, or empty stem. Calibration on the 20 hand-audited items: 17 of 20 agree on faithful versus not, against a pre-registered floor of 16 (the declared threshold grid gave 16 or 17 at every point), so the thresholds were kept. Census verdicts: 434 faithful, 502 not faithful, 19 unverified. Faithful fraction, faithful over faithful plus not faithful: Algebra~I $0.523$, Algebra~II $0.481$, geometry lower-central $0.357$, EQAO grade~6 $0.368$, geometry masked-stem $0.657$. Two post-hoc checks are disclosed and reported alongside the pre-registered subset: a strict variant that also drops any faithful item whose text layer carries a digit token absent from the transcription (85 of 434), and a locator repair that re-transcribed 12 Regents items and changed three verdicts; neither changes which rows clear, except that EQAO grade~6 does not clear on the strict subset ($5/10$). Rescoring reruns the catalog rule and reuses the saved per-item language-model predictions with the same bootstraps as the main text (item bootstrap seed 11, 1{,}000 replicates; cluster bootstrap seed 20260916, 2{,}000 replicates; language-model percentile bootstrap seeded by $21+n$, 1{,}000 replicates). Wall time on one GPU: 26 minutes for the census.

\subsection{Standards split}
\label{app:standards}

Tags: the ``Map to the Learning Standards'' table of the NYSED rating guide for each of the 58 Algebra~I and Algebra~II administrations in the masked-stem primary population, read from the PDF text layer (50 guides, 518 items), from a glyph table for three guides whose Calibri subset has a broken character map (30 items), or by template matching of rendered glyphs against six text-layer guides for five guides with no character map at all (57 items; 0 errors on 33{,}704 held-out characters). Every parsed table is validated (questions 1 to $N$ contiguous, exactly 24 multiple-choice rows). All 358 Algebra~I and 248 Algebra~II items carry a cluster code; 33 distinct clusters are used. Cluster headings and standards were fetched from the CCSS site and stored with page hashes before classification. The verb-class rule, vocabulary, and classification of all 33 clusters were hashed before any prediction was joined to a tag:
\shahex{c87a5ae0d897b389dbf5cda52086c1754955f7cbb5f9e7a7382f5ac7cc475488}.

Rule: each imperative verb heading a top-level clause of the cluster heading is execution (add, apply a rule, build, calculate, complete the square, compute, construct an equation or model, create, derive, evaluate an expression, factor, find, fit, graph, perform arithmetic operations, produce, rearrange, represent as a graph or table, rewrite, solve, summarize data, transform, translate between forms, write) or recognition or interpretation (analyze, choose, compare, construct an argument, decide, describe, distinguish, evaluate a process or claim, explain, identify, interpret, justify, know, make an inference, prove, reason, recognize, relate, understand). A purpose clause ``use $X$ to $V$'' takes the class of $V$; ``extend'' and ``use $X$'' with no purpose verb are unclassified, and a heading with no classifiable verb takes the class of its non-plus numbered standards. All execution: execution; all recognition: recognition or interpretation; both: mixed. Algebra~I: execution 164 items (clusters A-APR.A, A-CED.A, A-REI.B, A-REI.C, A-REI.D, A-SSE.B, F-BF.A, F-BF.B), recognition or interpretation 151 (A-APR.B, A-REI.A, A-SSE.A, F-IF.A, F-IF.B, F-IF.C, F-LE.B, N-RN.B, S-ID.C), mixed 43 (F-LE.A, N-Q.A, S-ID.A, S-ID.B). Algebra~II: 120, 93, and 35. A disclosed stricter execution class, which also requires that no numbered standard under the cluster be purely recognition, has 133 Algebra~I items (66 print-faithful) and does not clear on any model on the text layer either. The text-layer and filtered rows use the saved per-item predictions and the same bootstrap as the main text; the full-cell and faithful rows reproduce the text-layer and print-faithful rows exactly. On the repaired layer the same class table is joined to the repaired scores: Algebra~I verified 130 execution, 105 recognition or interpretation, 26 mixed; Algebra~II verified 83, 57, and 27.

\subsection{Item-level demand coding}
\label{app:coders}

Two language-model coders, GLM 5.2 and Kimi K3, each coded the 358 Algebra~I withheld-quantity items under one fixed rubric with three codes: execution (to pick the key you must operate on the specific given quantities: solve, evaluate, compute, factor with these coefficients, rewrite this expression, create an equation from these numbers, graph from this equation, find this value), recognition or interpretation (you can pick the key by recognizing or interpreting the form, meaning, or property named in the wording without computing on the given numbers), and mixed (needs both, or the key needs a small computation that any option-form reading would also give). Each coder received only the item id, stem, and options, with the key, every prediction, and every score field stripped by a helper script, and neither read the cluster split or the other coder's output. Each coder's README records the rubric verbatim with its sha256 (\shahex{ae93b7c651614dd622bba6ff4a9eddbe43c69b4b9ff67407d245365c18b11446} for the three rubric lines joined by newlines; \shahex{434ed271810ece24b0aa050e9424f0caaf7e3398d5654de7318a558505b6ef18} for the same lines as a bulleted text file). Item text came from the print-fidelity transcription where it existed (352 and 351 items) and from the text layer otherwise; the repaired layer did not yet exist when the coders ran. Coders could mark an item unreadable; GLM did so on two items whose options refer to a list absent from the transcribed text.

Codes: GLM 212 execution, 116 recognition or interpretation, 28 mixed, 2 unreadable; Kimi 249, 94, 15, 0; the cluster split 164, 151, 43. Cohen's $\kappa$ (unweighted, three classes, unreadable dropped): GLM versus Kimi $0.576$ ($n=356$, observed agreement $0.789$); GLM versus cluster $0.071$ ($0.461$); Kimi versus cluster $0.230$ ($0.564$). GLM against Kimi: 195 items both execution, 82 both recognition or interpretation, 4 both mixed; 32 items GLM recognition and Kimi execution, 8 the reverse. Against the cluster tag, 86 of the 151 recognition-tagged items are coded execution by GLM and 86 by Kimi (79 by both), and 41 (GLM) and 18 (Kimi) of the 164 execution-tagged items are coded recognition or interpretation. Subsets used in Table~\ref{tab:verbclass}: both coders execution 195 items (142 repaired-verified), both recognition 82 (61), both coders and the tag execution 97 (79), both coders and the tag recognition 51 (35). Rows are recomputed from the saved repaired-layer scores with the bootstrap above; the script and the per-subset id lists are in the export.
