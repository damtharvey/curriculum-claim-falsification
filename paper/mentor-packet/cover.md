Released items carry published tags: a curriculum code, a knowledge-and-understanding label, a TIMSS cognitive domain. A passing score is often read as evidence of that competency. The draft looks for programs that lack the tagged operation and still match the published key. The claim is one-sided: such a pass is evidence that passing does not require the tagged operation; a program that fails proves nothing beyond the programs we tried. The paper does not claim that students failed to learn the skill.

The authors are asking for a read and unstructured feedback, or a short review if that is more natural. Marks on the PDF, a few bullets, or a paragraph are all useful. Comments tonight would help if you have time. You do not have to comment, and comments that arrive later do not change the talk.

If you want a place to focus, one line is enough: what would make you trust or distrust that one-sided claim.
