# Weaknesses without excuse

The subset of the Limitations (`paper/limitations.tex`, framing pass on f5fb3d4) that a reasonable reviewer would say should have been done with the tools already in the repository. Judgments live here, not in the paper; the paper states each limitation plainly with what it affects and what would fix it.

Candidates judged and kept out of this list, with the reason: template-memorization test (needs authored novel items, a study of its own); EQAO and geometry replication (needs administrations outside the census); STAAR replication (the missing forms were searched for and not found); instruction-only channel (needs curriculum keys that are not public); ingestion gaps (mechanical, no claim rests on them).

| # | Weakness | Why it should have been done | Cost | Doable today, before the talk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | No human coding of item demand, even on a sample | The stronger statement (a pass on execution items does not require the operation) is decided on the both-coders execution subset, and the coders are two language models whose agreement with the tag is kappa 0.071 and 0.230. A 40-item human coding under the frozen rubric would tell us whether the coder classes are usable at all. The rubric, the blinded item sheets, and the helper script that strips keys and predictions already exist (Appendix A.7). | One person, about two hours for 40 items; two people for an agreement figure. | Yes, if a rater is available; the sheet can be generated from the existing helper in minutes. A 40-item sample does not fix the classes but does calibrate the coders. |
| 2 | One VLM family for both verification transcriptions | The claimed language-model population is the 431 items two readings of Qwen2-VL-7B agree on. A second reading by a different model family was the obvious independent check; the transcription harness, crops, normalization, and thresholds are written and hashed, and only the model call changes. | One pass over 606 page crops with a second local VLM, on the order of one GPU-hour, plus the comparison already coded. | Yes. Run the second family, recompute the verified set as items where the two families agree, and report the masked-stem rows on both populations. |
| 3 | Qwen 14B text-layer isomorph arms below the reproduction floor, cause undocumented | The floor was missed twice at 592/606 and the 14 disagreeing items were exported without diagnosis. Batch size 1 was already tried; the remaining causes (dtype, attention kernel, tokenizer version) are a short experiment. The repaired-layer control covers 14B from one load, so this affects only the audit trail, but an undiagnosed nondeterminism in the scorer is the kind of loose end a reviewer pulls. | Re-score the 14 items under two or three settings; under an hour of GPU and an afternoon of reading. | Yes. Either document the cause or re-run the text-layer arms from a single model load as was done on the repaired layer. |
| 4 | One human rater on the masked-stem sheet | A 40-item sheet with one rater at 15/40 cannot say whether a human without the givens reads the form cue; a second rater and a larger sheet were cheap and the sheet already exists. | One to two hours per rater; sizing the sheet to detect a rate near 0.5 against 0.25 needs roughly 40 to 60 items per rater. | Yes for a second rater on the existing sheet; a resized sheet is a few more hours of item selection. |
| 5 | Constructed-response Regents parts not scored for even one channel | The Regents finding is a fact about the multiple-choice part of each exam. The rating guides publish keys for the numeric-answer constructed-response parts, and value matching against those keys is executable with the unbound-formula rules already in the catalog family. Rubric-graded explanations are a different problem and are not flagged here. | Ingest the constructed-response parts and keys from the rating guides already parsed for cluster tags (one to two days of parsing), then run the existing catalog rules. | Partially. Ingestion of the numeric-answer parts and a catalog pass is feasible before the talk if started now; a rubric executor for explanation items is not. |

Not doable before the talk under any budget: authoring novel template items (memorization test), obtaining unreleased STAAR forms, curriculum keys for the instruction-only channel.
