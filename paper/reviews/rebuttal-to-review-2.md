**1. Item-level tag coding.** All 358 Algebra I withheld-quantity items were coded twice, by two language-model coders (GLM 5.2 and Kimi K3) working independently under one fixed three-code rubric (execution on the given quantities; recognition or interpretation of a form; mixed), each blind to the key, to every model prediction, and to the cluster split (Appendix A.7).
