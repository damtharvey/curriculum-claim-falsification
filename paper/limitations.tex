\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

We did not measure students. A witness is a fact about a key and a program. It is not a claim that students use the program.

The one-sided claim covers only the matched subset of each claimed cell: the 18 of 38 EQAO grade~6 items and the 80 of 246 New York Regents geometry items on which the lower-central rule returns the published key on the page-read options; the repaired-verified Algebra~I and Algebra~II items on which a masked-stem model returns the key; and the 14 of 15 Algebra~I displayed-equation items on which unique substitution returns the key. A pass on an item the program does not match has not been shown to omit the tagged operation.

We did not exhaust the class of programs that lack the tagged operation. A later search can find a TIMSS witness this one missed. On the withheld-quantity population, no channel in this file clears TIMSS knowing, applying, or reasoning. Public selected-response $n$ is about 50 to 80, and the clustered 80\% floors are $0.415$ to $0.441$, so a bypass rate in the low thirties would not have been seen. That null is power-limited and is not a certification of the tags.

Instruction-only (worked examples from the same unit) is specified in the protocol and was not run: curriculum scoring keys were not in the public files we could ingest.

The measurement instrument for the page is a vision-language model. About half of the census text layer in the claimed cells is not what students saw: of 955 items, 434 are print-faithful, 502 are not, and 19 could not be verified. The language-model claims are therefore made on a repaired layer in which the stem and options of every Algebra~I and Algebra~II item are the model's transcription of the page, verified by a second transcription. Both transcriptions come from one model and one revision (Qwen2-VL-7B-Instruct), with a different prompt and crop; an error the model makes the same way under both prompts passes verification, and the check has no human reading of the 606 pages beyond the 20-item audit (17 of 20 agree with the mechanical filter). Of 606 items, 175 do not verify and are scored only in the repaired-all sensitivity, which clears the same bars; 15 have no usable transcription and are not scored on this layer. The two catalog rows use one transcription without a second reading. Four Algebra items whose option perturbation raised or timed out are outside the isomorph rows. The verified population is not a random sample of the cell either: it is the items two readings of the page agree on, $0.73$ of Algebra~I and $0.68$ of Algebra~II, and a hard-to-read item is more likely to be outside it.

The EQAO grade~6 and geometry catalog rows are per-cell existence rows on the page-read options ($18/38$, interval $[0.316, 0.632]$; $80/246$, interval $[0.264, 0.382]$). Neither survives Holm over the 105 a priori tests. The EQAO row is specific to the pre-registered lower-central index, and under the earlier filter it had cleared on 14 items and failed a post-hoc stricter fidelity cut ($5/10$). Both are replication candidates.

STAAR grade~5 closure on the frozen 2019/2021/2022 cell is a discovery-set Holm reject ($21/40=0.525$) that did not replicate on held-out 2013/2014/2016/2017/2018 administrations after a pre-registered option-leak repair (combined $21/64=0.328$, CI $[0.219, 0.438]$, $n\ge 46$). Thirteen of 40 discovery stems contain artifact numerals. The artifact-free discovery rate $16/27$ is on a population defined after the fact.

Text-layer backsolving does not reach $n\ge 10$ fired on any cell. The image addendum measures it on 15 of 598 Algebra~I solving-tagged items (coverage $0.025$); that is not a census of Algebra~I. The row is not a 67-family Holm test.

The language-model channel has the operations. What is withheld is information: the stem, or every given quantity. The inference is that the published key is determined by question type plus the option set, not that the model solved the tagged exercise and not that a student would match the key from that slice. The model is not a student. Items on which the masker withheld nothing are excluded from that claim; TIMSS knowing's first-run rate was partly those items. The Algebra~I and Algebra~II witnesses hold on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct, and Phi-4 on the repaired-verified population and on the full repaired population. Neither holds on Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3 on the text layer, whose with-stem ceiling matches its masked-stem rate, so that miss is a weak solver; Mistral was not rescored on the repaired layer. Geometry on Phi-4 meets the text-layer cell bar but not the non-modal-key split and is not claimed; Qwen 7B clears TEKS grade~5 and grade~8 on that scorer only, and those rows are not claimed.

What the item asks is measured two ways and the two disagree. The publisher's cluster tag, classified by its heading verbs, puts 164 Algebra~I items in an execution class, and that class clears on all three models on the repaired-verified population. Two item-level coders, both language models rather than humans, agree with each other at $\kappa=0.576$ and with the tag at $\kappa=0.071$ and $0.230$; they code 212 and 249 items execution and read execution demand into 79 recognition-tagged items. On the 142 verified items both coders code execution, Qwen 7B and 14B clear and Phi-4 clears chance but not the modal-letter frequency. We therefore do not state that a pass on execution items omits the tagged operation; we state that on the matched items a pass does not require the given quantities. The coders' rubric is fixed and hashed, but the coders were not validated against human raters, and a human coding could move items between classes.

The option-numeral control on the repaired layer leaves a form component in all three scorers on both cells: every rank-preserving rate clears the bar. It also shows a numeral-specific component of about $0.1$ on Algebra~I for Qwen 14B and Phi-4 (paired drops $-0.103$ and $-0.090$ with intervals excluding zero) that the control cannot split between memorized option values and value cues that survive the masking. The earlier probes and this control together do not support memorization of the items as the whole effect; they do not rule out a memorized share. On the text layer the same control excluded Qwen 14B by a reproduction floor; that exclusion no longer bears on the claim, and the text-layer rows stay in the appendix as the record.

We did not ingest every public paper. English forms from 2019, 2021, and 2022 are the discovery STAAR census. Earlier English STAAR forms scored here are a replication set, not additional census years. Most NYSED 2018 graphic stems, TIMSS 2007, and later restricted TIMSS cycles are out of this file.

Constructed-response items are not headline cells. Scoring guides that reduce to a value are matching; explanations were not graded here.
