\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

We did not measure students. A witness is a fact about a key and a deficient policy: it is not a claim that students use the policy, and the pass rate of a deficient family on a cell is a vulnerability measure meaningful only relative to that documented family, never a probability that a student lacks the competency. The one-sided claim covers only the items a claimed program matches; a pass on an item the program does not match has not been shown to omit anything.

We did not exhaust the class of programs that lack the tagged operation, so a later search can find a TIMSS witness this one missed, and instruction-only (worked examples from the same unit) is specified in the protocol and was not run.

The instrument that reads the page is one vision-language model at one revision, under two prompts and crops. An error it makes the same way under both prompts passes verification, and no human read the 606 algebra pages beyond the 20-item audit. The two catalog rows use one transcription without a second reading. The verified population is the items two readings of the page agree on, and a hard-to-read item is more likely to be outside it.

What an Algebra~I item asks is measured two ways and the two disagree. The item-level coders are language models, not humans, and were not validated against human raters, so a disagreement between coder and tag can be a coder error as easily as a tag error; automated tagging of item demand has that record elsewhere, where language-model Q-matrices for a reading test agreed with a validated expert Q-matrix at $\kappa$ up to $0.63$, with wide variation across models and lower agreement on a later re-run~\cite{Fan_Bialo_2026}. A human coding could move items between classes and is the measurement that would settle whether a pass on execution items omits the tagged operation.

The option-numeral control rules out memorization of specific values: an item whose key was recalled by its printed numbers would lose its match when every option numeral changes. It does not rule out memorization of the recurring templates of Regents items, in which a stem type is paired with an option set of a fixed shape administration after administration, and released forms are widely mirrored online; a reader that had learned those templates would keep its rate under a change of values, exactly as the form component does. The flat administration-year probe speaks against a template that drifted over time and leaves a stable template untouched. The test that would separate a learned template from a property of the item form is novel items written to the same templates that no model has seen, in the manner of a benchmark rewritten in the style of a public one to measure the drop~\cite{zhang2024gsm1k}; this census has no such items.

We did not ingest every public paper. English STAAR forms from 2019, 2021, and 2022 are the discovery census and earlier English forms are a replication set, not additional census years; most NYSED 2018 graphic stems and TIMSS 2007 are out of this file. Constructed-response items are not headline cells: scoring guides that reduce to a value are matching, and explanations were not graded.
