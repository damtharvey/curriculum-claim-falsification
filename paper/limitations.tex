\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

We did not measure students. A witness is a fact about a key and a program. It is not a claim that students use the program.

The one-sided claim covers only the matched subset of each claimed cell: the 90 of 279 New York Regents geometry items and the 20 of 40 EQAO grade~6 items on which the lower-central rule returns the published key; the Algebra~I and Algebra~II items on which the masked-stem model returns the key; and the 14 of 15 Algebra~I displayed-equation items on which unique substitution returns the key. A pass on an item the program does not match has not been shown to omit the tagged operation.

We did not exhaust the class of programs that lack the tagged operation. A later search can find a TIMSS witness this one missed. On the withheld-quantity population, no channel in this file clears TIMSS knowing, applying, or reasoning. Public selected-response $n$ is about 50 to 80. That null is not a certification of the tags.

Instruction-only (worked examples from the same unit) is specified in the protocol and was not run: curriculum scoring keys were not in the public files we could ingest.

A 20-item printed-page audit of 10 geometry lower-central hits and 10 Algebra~I masked-stem hits found 8 matches of stem, options, and key, 8 glue cases, 3 truncations, and 1 wrong option set (Algebra~I June 2023 item 3). Rates in this paper are computed on the text layer. A witness on a corrupted option set does not refute the printed tag.

The EQAO grade~6 catalog witness is specific to the pre-registered lower-central index. Upper-central on that cell is chance.

STAAR grade~5 closure on the frozen 2019/2021/2022 cell is a discovery-set Holm reject ($21/40=0.525$) that did not replicate on held-out 2013/2014/2016/2017/2018 administrations after a pre-registered option-leak repair (combined $21/64=0.328$, CI $[0.219, 0.438]$, $n\ge 46$). Thirteen of 40 discovery stems contain artifact numerals. The artifact-free discovery rate $16/27$ is on a population defined after the fact.

Text-layer backsolving does not reach $n\ge 10$ fired on any cell. The image addendum measures it on 15 of 598 Algebra~I solving-tagged items (coverage $0.025$); that is not a census of Algebra~I. The row is not a 67-family Holm test.

The language-model channel has the operations. What is withheld is information: the stem, or every given quantity. The inference is that the published key is determined by question type plus the option set, not that the model solved the tagged exercise and not that a student would match the key from that slice. The model is not a student. Items on which the masker withheld nothing are excluded from that claim; TIMSS knowing's first-run rate was partly those items. The Algebra~I and Algebra~II witnesses hold on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct, and Phi-4. Geometry on Phi-4 meets the cell bar ($0.404$, $n=166$, lower CI above chance and above modal B $0.289$) but not the non-modal-key split ($0.229$, $n=118$) and is not claimed; it does not hold on Qwen or Mistral at the same bar. None of those cells hold on Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3. Mistral's with-stem ceiling matches its masked-stem rate, so that miss is a weak solver. Qwen 7B also clears TEKS grade~5 and grade~8 at the registered channel bar on that scorer only; Qwen 14B and Phi-4 do not, so those rows are not claimed. On Qwen 14B the Algebra~I leftover is strongest on expression and text options and is weak on numeric options; Phi-4 also clears chance on numeric options ($0.366$, $n=71$).

We did not ingest every public paper. English forms from 2019, 2021, and 2022 are the discovery STAAR census. Earlier English STAAR forms scored here are a replication set, not additional census years. Most NYSED 2018 graphic stems, TIMSS 2007, and later restricted TIMSS cycles are out of this file.

Constructed-response items are not headline cells. Scoring guides that reduce to a value are matching; explanations were not graded here.
